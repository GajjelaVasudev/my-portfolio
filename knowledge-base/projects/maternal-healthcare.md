## Overview
- **Project Title**: Maternal Healthcare Platform (NurtureConnect)
- **Role**: Backend Developer & Systems Architect
- **Year**: 2023
- **Repository URL**: https://github.com/GajjelaVasudev
- **Category**: Clinical Workflow & Healthcare Systems

## Problem Statement
Maternal healthcare tracking in decentralized clinics often suffers from fragmented patient records, missing immunization milestones, and lack of audit trails for clinical decisions.

## Architectural Solution
Constructed a role-based clinical coordination platform supporting longitudinal patient care pathways, prenatal milestones, and structured clinical notes with strict audit logging.

## Key Technical Features
- **Role-Based Access Control (RBAC)**: Strict permission boundaries separating obstetricians, triage nurses, and patient views to ensure HIPAA-level privacy standards.
- **Longitudinal Patient Timeline**: Chronological milestone tracking for prenatal screenings, vaccinations, and lab diagnostics.
- **Audit Logging**: Immutable event log tracking every clinical record view, mutation, and referral.

## Tech Stack
- **Backend**: Node.js, Express
- **Database**: MongoDB with schema validation
- **Security**: RBAC enforcement, input sanitization, and cryptographic password hashing
