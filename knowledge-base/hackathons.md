### Project Summary
Built **DLONE (DLE Sarthi)**, an explainable digital assistance platform designed specifically around the practical daily workflow needs of rural women entrepreneurs working as Direct-to-Consumer Livelihood Entrepreneurs (DLEs).

### Verified Features & Technical Contributions
- **Hyperlocal Recommendations with Explanations**: Surfaces localized inventory suggestions and recommended business actions with transparent, clear explanations for why each action was suggested.
- **Grounded AI Pitch Generator**: An AI sales-pitch assistant strictly grounded in authentic product details to prevent unsupported answers or hallucinations.
- **Monthly Essentials Reminders**: Recurring automated alerts and reminders for essential household supplies to enable timely reordering and avoid stockouts.
- **Product-Defect Resolution Workflow**: A dedicated dispute and replacement pipeline created to protect rural entrepreneurs from lost sales and protect their commissions.
- **Multilingual Support & Peer Benchmarking**: Multilingual user interface supporting English, Odia, Hindi, and Telugu, accompanied by localized peer benchmarking metrics.
- **Backend Safeguards & Security**: Implemented PostgreSQL row-level security (RLS) policies, PII sanitization of survey data responses, and a comprehensive 30-case automated test suite.
